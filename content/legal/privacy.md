# Align Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 May 2026. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

## 1. Who we are

ALIGN LIFESTYLE PTE. LTD. ("Align", "we", "us", or "our") delivers AI-driven speech rehearsal tailored for working professionals. Our platform enables you to practice challenging workplace interactions alongside a conversational AI counterparty and obtain detailed feedback following each session.

This Privacy Policy outlines the personal data we gather, our processing practices, external disclosures, and the options available to you. This policy applies to your use of our website and app at rehearse.livealign.co and livealign.co.

If you have questions regarding your personal details, please reach out to us at [privacy@livealign.co](mailto:privacy@livealign.co). You can find complete corporate disclosures in section 14.

## 2. Scope and applicable law

Align is operated from Singapore and made available globally. We handle personal data in line with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 ("PDPA"), the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”), the UK General Data Protection Regulation (“UK GDPR”)   and any other data protection laws that apply to us.

This policy is written to give all users a clear, equivalent set of rights regardless of where they are located.

## 3. Data we collect

When you use Align, we may collect the following types of personal data:

### Account data

Information you give us to create and manage your account, such as your contact details (such as email address and name) and sign-in information.

### Rehearsal data

Information you give us to set up a rehearsal, the written record of each rehearsal, the feedback we generate for you, and any feedback you give us afterwards. Audio is processed in real time and is not stored by Align.

### Sensitive information

Your rehearsals may include sensitive information if you choose to share it, for example about your health (including mental health, disability or pregnancy), racial or ethnic origin, religious or philosophical beliefs, sex life or sexual orientation, gender identity, political opinions or trade union membership. You never have to include it.

If you do choose to share sensitive information during rehearsals, we ask for your explicit consent before you start rehearsing. With that consent, our AI uses the information only to respond to you during the rehearsal and to give you feedback afterwards. It is saved, encrypted, with your rehearsal. Our team cannot read your rehearsals.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the rehearsals concerned from Past rehearsals, or by deleting your account.

### Your workplace and other people

Align works best when you describe your real situation. Please don't share confidential employer information, such as trade secrets or passwords. Please also leave out personal details about other people that the rehearsal doesn't need. A first name and a role is usually enough. If you mention other people, that information is used automatically by our AI to run your rehearsal and give you feedback, and is saved encrypted with your rehearsal. We do this based on our legitimate interest in providing the service. Our team cannot read your rehearsals.

### Purchase and usage data

Information about the rehearsals you buy, your payments, and how many free rehearsals and credits you have left.

### Technical data

Information about your device, browser and connection, and analytical data on how you use Align. This includes information collected through cookies and similar technologies (see section 5).

We do not currently use advertising trackers or marketing cookies. If we add any in future, we will update this policy and request your consent where required.

## 4. How we use your data

We use your personal data to:

- Provide the rehearsal service
- Authenticate your account and keep it secure

- Save your rehearsal history so you can review past sessions
- Understand product usage and improve the service

- Respond to support requests
- Comply with legal obligations

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, section 9 sets out the legal basis we rely on for each of these uses.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your rehearsal transcripts to train AI models.

## 5. Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies keep you signed in and keep your account secure. They are always on, because the service doesn't work without them.

Analytics cookies help us understand how Align is used so we can improve it. We use two analytics tools; PostHog, to count page views and actions, record how people move through the app and where they click (with all text hidden), find and fix errors, test new features, and run short in app surveys. PostHog never receives the content of your rehearsals, and PostHog data is stored in the United States. Google Analytics 4 (provided by Google LLC, US)  which records things like pages viewed, approximate location, device type, and overall usage patterns. Its data is stored in the United States.

Our [Cookie Policy](/cookies) explains this in more detail.

Analytics only runs if you agree to it. When you first visit, a banner asks you to choose:

- Accept all: essential cookies plus analytics
- Essential only: essential cookies only. No analytics runs.

We remember your choice in your browser for 12 months. You can change it at any time with the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page.

We do not use advertising or marketing cookies.

## 7. International transfers

Align operates from Singapore, and most of our service providers are based in the United States. Your account and rehearsal data are stored on servers in Ireland, in the European Union. However, our voice and AI providers process your rehearsals in the United States in real time, and our team in Singapore can access your data where needed to run the service. Analytics data from PostHog and Google Analytics is stored in the United States.

Because we run Align from Singapore and use providers in other countries, your data may be transferred to, or accessed from, countries outside the one you live in. When your data is transferred across borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards. For transfers from the EU and UK, we use the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum for UK users), or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified under it. For transfers from Singapore, we meet the transfer requirements of the PDPA through our data processing agreements with each provider, which require them to protect your data to a standard comparable to the PDPA. You can request a copy of these safeguards by emailing [privacy@livealign.co](mailto:privacy@livealign.co).

## 8. Data retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, or for as long as the law requires.

- **Account data:** kept while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days after you close your account, except where we are legally required to keep certain records for longer (for example, payment records, which we keep for 10 years from the end of the year of the transaction for tax purposes).
- **Rehearsal data** (transcripts, situation descriptions, and your answers after each rehearsal): kept for as long as your account is open, so you can access them from Past rehearsals. Deleted within 30 days after you close your account, or within 30 days of you deleting them from Past rehearsals.

- **Server logs:** kept for up to 90 days for security purposes.
- **Analytics data:** Only for visitors who accepted analytics cookies.

- PostHog usage events, heatmaps, error reports and survey answers are kept for up to 7 years. If you delete your account, we delete your analytics data from PostHog within 30 days.
- PostHog session recordings are kept for 30 days from the date they are recorded.

- Google Analytics data  is kept for 14 months from your last visit. Reports showing only combined totals for all visitors may be kept longer, as they do not identify anyone.

## 9. Legal bases for processing

The table below shows the legal basis we rely on for each purpose identified in section 4.

| What we use your data for | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Provide the rehearsal service | Performance of our contract with you (contractual necessity). For sensitive information you choose to include, your explicit consent. |
| Authenticate your account and keep it secure | Performance of our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in keeping Align and its users safe from misuse. |
| Save your rehearsal history so you can review past sessions | Performance of our contract with you. |
| Understand product usage and improve the service | Your consent, given through the cookie banner. For feedback you give us after a rehearsal, our legitimate interest in improving Align. |
| Respond to support requests | Performance of our contract with you. For sensitive information you choose to include, your explicit consent |
| Comply with legal obligations | Compliance with a legal obligation. |

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

We need your account and rehearsal data to provide the service. If you choose not to give it to us, we cannot run rehearsals for you.

## 10. Your rights

Regardless of where you are located, you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete data

- Delete your data (the "right to be forgotten" or right to erasure)
- Restrict processing in certain circumstances.

- Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Receive your data in a portable format (data portability)

- Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent as the legal basis
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at [privacy@livealign.co](mailto:privacy@livealign.co). We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

We will respond within one month (or 30 days) from the day we receive your request. If a request is complex, the law may allow us more time. If we need it, we will tell you within the first month and explain why.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us first by emailing [privacy@livealign.co](mailto:privacy@livealign.co). We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and respond without undue delay. You can also complain to a data protection authority at any time.

In Singapore, you may also contact the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) at [https://www.pdpc.gov.sg](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg). Users in the EU may contact their national data protection authority. Users in the UK may contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [https://ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## 11. Security

We protect your data using industry-standard security measures, including:

- Encryption
- Access controls

- Regular security updates

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach we will notify the relevant authorities and, where required, you, in line with our legal obligations.

## 12. Children
