Align provides AI-powered roleplay rehearsal for difficult workplace conversations. You  select a scenario, describe your  situation, and rehearse the conversation with an AI character voiced through speech synthesis. The AI also gives optional guidance during a rehearsal when you say "I'm stuck" or press the “I’m stuck” button, and a structured debrief after each session.
