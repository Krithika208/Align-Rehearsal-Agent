# Align Cookie Policy

Updated on 20 November 2026

This policy explains how ALIGN LIFESTYLE PTE. LTD. (“Align”, “we”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website (livealign.co) and our app (rehearse.livealign.co).

## Meaning
Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage. In this policy, “cookies” covers both.

## Usage of cookies
We use two types of cookies:

- Essential cookies keep you signed in, keep your account secure and remember your cookie choice. They are always on, because the service doesn't work without them.
- Analytics cookies help us understand how Align is used so we can improve it. They only run if you accept them.

We do not use advertising or marketing cookies.

## Analytics
We use two analytics tools in our app which are PostHog and Google Analytics 4. Neither runs unless you choose “Accept all”. If you choose “Essential only”, or make no choice, no analytics cookies or storage are written. If you later change to “Essential only”, analytics stops straight away and its cookies and storage are deleted.

PostHog helps us understand how people use the app. With your consent, we may use it to:

- Count page views and actions, such as starting a rehearsal, without recording anything you type.
- See how people move through the app and where they click, with all text hidden so we cannot see what you type.

- Find and fix technical errors, with page addresses removed.
- Test new features by showing different versions to different users.

- Show short in-app surveys, if you choose to reply.

PostHog never receives the content of your rehearsals, your name or your email address. It does not keep your IP address, only your approximate location. PostHog data is stored in the United States.

Google Analytics 4, provided by Google LLC in the United States, records things like pages viewed, approximate location, device type and overall usage pattern.

Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains how long we keep analytics data.

## Third-party content on our website
Our website includes a booking widget from Cal.com and a video from YouTube. These services may set their own cookies when you use the booking widget or play the video. Their own cookie policies explain how they use them.

## Your choices
When you first use our app, a banner lets you choose:

- Accept all: essential cookies plus analytics
- Essential only: essential cookies only. No analytics runs.

We remember your choice for 12 months. You can change it at any time using the “Cookie preferences” link at the bottom of every page.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, our website and app may not work properly.

## Changes to this policy
We will update this policy if the cookies we use change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
