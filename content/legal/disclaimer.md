# AI Coaching Disclaimer

Align provides AI-powered conversation rehearsal. Both the in-rehearsal tactical guidance (Teaching Mode) and the post-rehearsal debrief are generated by artificial intelligence informed by professional coaching principles.

## Align is not:

- A licensed therapist or counsellor
- Medical, mental health, or psychological care
- Legal advice
- A substitute for human coaching from a qualified professional
- Suitable for crisis situations or active mental health distress

## Align is:

A private space to practise difficult workplace conversations, get a fresh perspective on your approach, and try out new phrasing — before you have the real conversation.

The AI may produce errors, miss important context, or offer suggestions that aren't right for your specific situation. This applies to all AI-generated content in Align, including tactical guidance offered during the rehearsal itself. You remain responsible for any decisions you make in your real workplace and your real life.

## If you are in crisis or struggling with your mental health

Please reach out to a qualified professional. Below are some key resources:

**Singapore**
- **Samaritans of Singapore (SOS)**: call **1-767** (24/7) or visit sos.org.sg
- **National CARE Hotline**: **1800-202-6868**
- **Emergency:** **995**

**United States**
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**: call or text **988** (24/7)
- **Crisis Text Line**: text **HOME** to **741741**
- **Emergency:** **911**

**United Kingdom**
- **Samaritans**: call **116 123** (24/7) or visit samaritans.org
- **NHS**: call **111** or visit nhs.uk/mental-health
- **Emergency:** **999**

**Australia**
- **Lifeline**: call **13 11 14** (24/7) or visit lifeline.org.au
- **Beyond Blue**: **1300 22 4636**
- **Emergency:** **000**

**Canada**
- **Talk Suicide Canada**: call **1-833-456-4566** (24/7), or text **45645** in the evenings
- **Emergency:** **911**

**Anywhere else in the world**

For a directory of crisis helplines in your country, please visit **Befrienders Worldwide** at **befrienders.org** or the **International Association for Suicide Prevention** at **iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres**.

If you are in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services.
